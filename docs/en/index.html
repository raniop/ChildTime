THE LEARNING ITSELF

It adapts to your child

Not just another quiz app. Tofy knows what grade your child is in, what they're good at, and when it's time to level up.

🎒

Grade by grade

Every question is tagged to its grade, from kindergarten through 8th grade. Each September your child moves up a grade — and the questions move up with them.

📈

A level that adjusts itself

The level is set separately for each topic, based on real results and centered on the level you chose. Too easy? It goes up. Too hard? It comes down.

🔊

Even for kids who can't read yet

Questions and answers are read aloud, and the answers are numbered — so even the youngest kids can play on their own.

💛

A mistake isn't a failure

A wrong answer gets a hint and an explanation — never language about failing. Kids can also ask Mom or Dad for help, and you answer right from your phone.

🧹

House chores, too

Tidying their room or clearing the table can be worth play minutes. Your child marks a chore as done, and you approve it — sometimes with a photo.

🏆

Friends and a live quiz

Kids who connect with a friend code can compete on a leaderboard and in a real-time live quiz. No chat and no messages — and you can see and remove any friend.

Frequently asked questions

What ages is Tofy for?

Kindergarten through 8th grade. During setup you pick your child's grade, and questions are served right at that level. Younger kids who can't read yet can play too, with questions read aloud.

How is screen time actually blocked?

Through Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls). You choose which apps are locked, and they open only once your child has earned minutes. You can always unlock time manually from your phone.

Do we need two devices?

We recommend it: one device for your child and one for you. You can also connect several devices to the same child, and several parents to the same family — everything stays in sync.

Can my child buy something on their own?

No. Your child's device never shows prices. If they want a new world, they tap "Ask Mom or Dad" — and you decide.

Are there ads or tracking?

No. No ads, no advertising identifier, and no third-party analytics. Data is used only to save progress and sync between your family's devices. Read our Privacy Policy.

What about siblings?

One Tofy+ subscription unlocks every world for every child in the family. A single world is bought for one child, and it's half price for a brother or sister.

How much does it cost?

You can start for free. Tofy+ is a monthly or yearly family subscription; the price is shown in the app in your local currency, and you can cancel anytime from your Apple ID settings. Once your child really starts learning, we unlock Tofy+ for two weeks as a gift — no credit card and no automatic renewal.
