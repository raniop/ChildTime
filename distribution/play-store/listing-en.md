## Full description (≤4000)

```
Tofy turns your child's screen time into a learning game.

Your child picks a world, answers curriculum questions, and earns minutes to play in the apps you approved — YouTube, games, whatever you choose. They learn without being reminded, and they have a real reason to solve one more.

How it works
• You choose which apps stay locked until your child earns time
• Your child answers questions on a journey and walks away with minutes
• The better they do, the more stars they collect and worlds they open
• Tofy, a magical friend, is with them the whole way and cheers every win

More than 20 worlds
Math, reading comprehension, English, science, logic, geography, history, animals, space, the human body, flags, music, money and life skills, and more — from 1st through 8th grade, matched to each child's age and grade.

What makes it different
• Difficulty that adapts on its own, per child and per subject
• Zero frustration — no red, no "wrong"
• Surprise chests, answer streaks, a lucky wheel and mini-games
• Household chores: your child picks a reward, you approve it
• A friends leaderboard, and a live quiz against friends — no chat, no photos
• Four languages: English, עברית, Русский, العربية
• No ads

Any mix of devices
• Android phone or tablet, iPhone or iPad — parent and child can each be on a different one
• The parent's device is the control center: create a profile for each child, connect the child's device by scanning a QR code, and see live what each child is learning
• Several parents and several children in one family, with progress synced securely across every device

The lock on a child's Android device
To keep other apps locked until your child earns time, Tofy uses Android's Accessibility service. A parent turns it on, after a screen that explains exactly what it does. The service only sees which app is opening right now: it doesn't read what's on the screen, doesn't see messages or passwords, and app names are never stored or sent anywhere. The parent chooses what always stays open, and the phone app and home screen are never locked. It can be turned off at any time.

Safety and privacy
• Parent settings are protected by a 4-digit parent code
• A parent creates the family, gives consent, and sets up every child
• No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking
• Delete all your data at any time from parent settings

Tofy — because every question moves you forward.

Privacy Policy: https://tofyapp.com/privacy
Terms of Use: https://tofyapp.com/terms
```
